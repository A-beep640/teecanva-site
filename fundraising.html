How a Teecanva fundraiser works

No cash to collect, no inventory to store, no risk if orders are slow.

1

We design it

A one-of-a-kind design for your group, event, or cause — 1–2 concepts within 3 days.

2

You share the link

We give you a simple pre-order form to share. Supporters order and pay online directly — your group never handles cash.

3

We print & deliver

Bulk printed locally and delivered to your church, field, or event in about 2 weeks. You keep the margin above cost.

Example: 50 shirts at $12 each = $600 cost. Sell them at $20 each = $1,000. Your group keeps $400. Exact numbers depend on your design and quantities — every fundraiser starts with a free quote, and the $95 design fee is waived on orders of 50+ shirts.
