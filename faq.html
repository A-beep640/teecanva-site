Group shirt orders

What's the minimum order?

Screen printing starts at 12 shirts, but pricing gets meaningfully better at 24+ — that's where we recommend starting. Smaller than 12? Ask us about single-piece options.

How long does it take?

About two weeks from the day your order closes to delivery. Design concepts arrive within 3 days of your request. Need it faster? Rush production is available — mention your date in the order form.

How do payments work? Do I collect money?

No — that's the point of the full-stack service. We give you a simple order link to share with your group; each person orders and pays individually. You never front money or chase payments.

Can you match our school or team colors?

Yes. We offer Pantone color matching on bulk orders so your shirts hit the exact shades.

Where do you deliver?

Local delivery across Dallas–Fort Worth, including direct to your campus or event. Shipping elsewhere in the US is available on request.

What if we need a reorder later?

We keep your screens and artwork on file, so reorders are quick and skip the setup step entirely.

Custom design service

What's included in the $95 design fee?

One-on-one design work: 1–2 original concepts within 3 days, one revision round, and print-ready files. The fee is waived entirely on group orders of 50+ shirts.

Who owns the design?

You do. Full rights transfer to you on delivery — use it for shirts, banners, social posts, whatever you need.

Can you adapt one of your bundle designs for our group?

Absolutely — that's a popular option. We'll customize a bundle design with your school name, mascot, or colors as part of your group order.

Design bundles (Etsy)

What file formats do I get?

Every bundle includes SVG vectors plus transparent PNGs at 300 DPI — ready for Cricut, print-on-demand, or your local print shop.

What's the difference between personal and commercial licenses?

Personal covers shirts and gifts for yourself. Commercial lets you sell up to 200 physical end-products made with the designs — ideal for small POD sellers and team stores.

How do I receive my files?

Instant download from Etsy the moment your purchase completes. No waiting, no shipping.
