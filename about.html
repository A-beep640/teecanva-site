Tee + Canva = Teecanva

Teecanva started with a simple idea: original, well-designed shirts — not the generic clip-art stuff. Every design in our catalog is created from scratch, drawn for the people who'll actually wear it.

Today we've grown into a full custom apparel studio for the Dallas–Fort Worth area. We design group shirts for schools, teams, churches, and organizations — then handle the bulk printing and delivery ourselves, so organizers never touch inventory or chase payments.

Our design bundles give the same original artwork to DIY creators as instant downloads: print it yourself, or bring it to your local shop.

Abiuth Maronga

Founder & Designer

SPED Substitute Teacher with Dallas ISD and lifelong designer. Abiuth started Teecanva to make merch people are proud to wear — beginning with the educator community he works in every day.
